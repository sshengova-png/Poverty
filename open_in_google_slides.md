# How to open the presentation in Google Slides

Use this file:

`poverty_presentation_google_slides.pptx`

## Option 1: From Google Drive

1. Open Google Drive.
2. Click **New** -> **File upload**.
3. Upload `poverty_presentation_google_slides.pptx`.
4. Right-click the uploaded file.
5. Choose **Open with** -> **Google Slides**.

## Option 2: From Google Slides

1. Open <https://slides.google.com>.
2. Create a blank presentation.
3. Click **File** -> **Import slides**.
4. Go to **Upload**.
5. Upload `poverty_presentation_google_slides.pptx`.
6. Select all slides and import them.

The deck uses a 16:9 layout and Arial fonts to keep formatting stable after
Google Slides imports it.
